Signature method

Bad reviews are product intelligence.

The one-star reviews on competing products are a map. Each complaint is an unmet need — and an unmet need is where an optional improvement can earn its place.

Customer complaint

"The zip snagged and a mesh pocket tore within a week."

→
Unmet need

Durability people can trust — the parts that fail first, fixed first.

→
A better product idea

A sturdier zip pull and reinforced pockets — a change only worth it if the price supports it.

Complaints are paraphrased review themes about comparable products — treated as leads to investigate, not verified defect rates. Selling the original design can be the right answer.
