Questions

Straight answers.

What each service includes, what to send, and what we don't promise.

Are these reports about my own product?

Yes. Every paid report investigates the product you submit. Send your product photo and a purchase or supplier link (a description works to start), and we research that product.

What does the $49 package include?

Two product reports at the same research standard as the $29 Single Product Report, plus a side-by-side comparison and a recommendation on which to test first. It's two of your submitted products — not a pack of premade case reports. The comparison can conclude "neither is ready to test" when the evidence supports that.

What makes the $249 service different?

The Complete Business Launch Consultation adds in-depth research, an optional development concept and supplier brief, supplier screening and negotiation guidance, a startup budget and cash-flow plan, business finance suggestions, and a personalized consultation with a launch action plan — for one selected product.

Is funding included?

Consultation and finance suggestions are included in the $249 service. Capital and financing approval are not — availability and approval depend on the provider and your circumstances. SourceVerdict provides consultation, not funding.

Do I have to customize the product?

No. We evaluate selling it as sourced and optional improvements fairly, so you can choose the approach that fits your customer and budget. We don't claim an untested design commands a higher price.

Are supplier outreach and physical samples included?

The advertised services include the specified supplier screening and negotiation guidance. Goods, physical samples, third-party testing and executing sourcing on your behalf are not included. A supplier's listed MOQ alone does not determine whether to proceed, and negotiated quantities need actual supplier agreement.

What should I send?

A product photo and a purchase or supplier link, plus your business location, selling market, channel and approximate budget. If you have only one starting reference, send it and we'll confirm what else is needed before research begins.

What markets do you cover?

Currently the United States and Canada. Standard scope is one target market and one primary channel; the two-product package uses one shared market and channel. Different-market requests are confirmed before payment.

How do you estimate sales?

We use available demand evidence and research tools, with sources, dates and limitations stated. Competitor estimates are not a forecast or guarantee of your own sales.

What does GO mean? What does PASS mean?

GO — the evidence supports a defined next step, often a sample or small pilot with named conditions. PASS — on the current evidence it isn't worth the spend yet; the report explains what would need to change. A GO for a pilot is not a GO for inventory, and a supported PASS is a valid outcome.

How long does it take?

We confirm timing with you when we confirm the scope, rather than promise a fixed turnaround up front.

What happens after I submit?

We review your submission, confirm the scope, and send a secure payment link before any work begins. After payment we prepare and deliver your report(s) — and, for the $249 service, a personalized consultation. Payment and delivery are separate, verified steps.

Still have a question?

Send it over and we'll answer before you commit to anything.
