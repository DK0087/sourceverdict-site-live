Questions

Straight answers.

What each service includes, what to send, and what we don't promise.

What services do you offer?

A free Complete Sample Report (one existing worked example); a Custom Product Screen — $99 (a focused first assessment of your product); a Full Product Launch Report — $249 (a personalized launch plan with financial consultation); and Supplier Validation & Negotiation Support — from $750 (separately scoped outreach, quotations and negotiation). Prices are USD, before applicable taxes.

What should I send?

A product photo and a purchase or supplier link, plus your business location, target market, channel and approximate budget. If you have only one starting reference, send it and we'll confirm what else is needed.

Do I have to customize the product?

No. We compare selling it as sourced with optional changes, so you can choose an approach that fits your customer and budget.

Does a supplier's listed MOQ automatically mean PASS?

No. We explore practical pilot quantities and negotiation options. Final quantities and costs depend on supplier agreement and your budget.

What does financial consultation include?

The full report includes launch-budget planning, cash-flow assessment, relevant financing options and application-readiness guidance. The screen includes a brief funding-fit overview. Providers make their own eligibility and approval decisions — SourceVerdict provides consultation, not the loan.

Can I buy a report from a public case post?

Public cases demonstrate our method. We offer one free sample; paid investigations are prepared for the product you submit.

How do you estimate sales?

We use available demand evidence and research tools, with sources, dates and limitations stated. Competitor estimates are not a forecast or guarantee of your own sales.

What does GO mean? What does PASS mean?

GO — the evidence supports a defined next step, often a sample or small pilot with named conditions. PASS — on the current evidence this version isn't worth the spend yet; the report explains what would need to change. A GO for a pilot is not a GO for inventory, and a supported PASS is a valid outcome.

What markets do you cover?

Currently the United States and Canada. Each report is scoped to one target country and one primary channel; multiple markets need scope confirmation before payment.

Do you contact suppliers?

A Full Product Launch Report includes a supplier-message and negotiation kit for you to send. Actual outreach, comparable quotations, negotiation, document collection and sample coordination are a separate engagement — Supplier Validation & Negotiation Support, from $750. Samples, testing, inspection, freight and goods cost extra.

Does a report include certifications or compliance?

Where relevant, you get a preliminary requirements and document roadmap for your product and market. It's a starting point — not a lab report, certification, customs ruling or legal clearance.

How long does it take?

We confirm timing with you when we confirm scope, rather than promise a fixed turnaround up front.

What happens after I submit?

We review your submission, confirm the scope, and send a secure payment link before any work begins. After payment we prepare and deliver your investigation. Payment and delivery are separate, verified steps.

Still have a question?

Send it over and we'll answer before you commit to anything.
