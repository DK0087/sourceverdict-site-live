The verdict language

What GO and PASS mean.

GO

Worth a defined next step.

The evidence supports a specific action — often a sample or a small pilot, with named conditions. A GO for a pilot is not a GO for committing inventory.

PASS

Not on the current evidence.

This version isn't worth the spend yet — the report explains what would need to change. A supported PASS is a valid, useful outcome.

A GO or PASS applies to a defined action. A supplier's listed minimum order quantity does not automatically mean PASS — we explore practical pilot quantities and negotiation. Customization is never assumed.

Data & sources

Evidence first, with its limits.

We start from observable evidence — marketplace listings, retail pricing, reviews and supplier pages, each dated. We use research tools where available and permitted, and state sources, dates and limitations. Competitor sales estimates are not a forecast of your own sales, and we deliver commercial interpretation, not raw dashboards.

Important

Decision support — not a guarantee.

SourceVerdict reduces uncertainty before you commit money. It does not guarantee sales, margins, supplier concessions, financing approval or regulatory clearance. Financial consultation is our own advice; providers make their own eligibility and approval decisions. Products with specialised safety, medical, food or electrical requirements may need a different scope.
